# Optical chronology study

The history, original photographs, keyboard/wheel controller, reading dialogue and other exhibition sections are preserved. Only the desktop chronology presentation is changed.

## Scene reconstruction
The composition is a visual study from a recorded reference, not the original studio's source code or proprietary assets. The frontal board uses47% viewport width and62% height, centred at37.5%x44%. The spiral projection is adjusted around half-progress to keep both outgoing and incoming boards substantial and visible; the native chronology input/easing/history controller remains unchanged.

## Materials
The central decorative glass sculpture is built in Blender and rendered offline. The surrounding unequal particle clusters are procedurally authored offline. They are not historical objects, geographic reconstructions, or images attributed to Li Chengsan.

The delivered version loads one transparent sculpture image and one precombined cloud image. Alternate views and separated sprite sources are retained for reproducibility but are not animated at runtime. This avoids the rendering cost of live refraction, multiple moving full-screen particle textures and alpha crossfades. Pointer-following highlights on the boards remain interactive.

This is not a real-time volumetric/refraction renderer, nor a claim of pixel-perfect reproduction. Static material detail is balanced against readable historical content and continuous scrolling on desktop. Runtime React Bits GlassSurface refraction is not enabled in this preview; retained notices belong to earlier material studies.
